## ICR verdict
Current working ICR remains 1:15. This review does not support winding back to 1:20 and does not yet justify strengthening the ratio either.

Reasoning:
- Dexcom hourly statistics show 0% low and 0% very low from 12:00 through midnight across the review period, so there is no clear post-meal low signal suggesting 1:15 is too strong.
- Most low/very-low time is overnight or early morning. The 27 Aug overnight low period was explicitly documented as a faulty-sensor exception with finger-prick glucose 8.0 mmol/L.
- The main adverse pattern remains higher glucose later in the day/evening, particularly around high-fat/high-carb meals and closely spaced food/insulin events.
- Several recent meals are not clean single-meal ICR tests because meals, sweets or additional food overlap within the 4-6 hour review window.
- Whole-unit round-up can make small-carbohydrate meals effectively stronger than 1:15, so those cases need separate safety monitoring rather than being used to change the global ratio.
