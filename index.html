What is working
TIR is 85%, GMI 6.6% and CV 28.8%, all comfortably inside the Dexcom/AGP goals. Dexcom shows 0% low and 0% very low from 12:00 through midnight across this review period.
What to watch
The weaker area is later in the day. High time reaches 22% at 21:00-22:00 and 27% at 22:00-23:00. High-fat/high-carb and closely spaced meals remain more plausible drivers than a simple global ICR failure.
Data quality
The 27 Aug overnight low period remains a documented faulty-sensor exception. Some 1 Sep app and Dexcom insulin entries do not match perfectly, so those meals are excluded from clean ratio-setting evidence.

What I've learned about Darren's diabetes

PROVEN REPEATEDLYTime in range has stayed well above the 70% goal across recent reviews.
LIKELY1:15 remains the best-supported working ICR. This review shows no clear post-meal low pattern suggesting it is too strong.
LIKELYHigh-fat/high-carb and closely spaced evening eating are more likely to produce delayed or extended rises.
UNDER INVESTIGATIONBread and other rapid starch meals may have their own response pattern, but the current evidence is not clean enough to justify changing the global ICR.
ANALYSIS RULESmall-carb meals rounded up to a whole insulin unit can be effectively much stronger than 1:15 and must be monitored separately rather than used to judge the global ratio.
ANALYSIS RULEOverlapping meals, unmatched Dexcom doses, documented sensor faults and unclear nutrition are excluded from clean ICR evidence.
