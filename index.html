What is working

What to watch
The weaker area is later in the day. High time reaches 22% at 21:00-22:00 and 27% at 22:00-23:00. High-fat/high-carb and closely spaced meals remain more plausible drivers than a simple global ICR failure.
Data quality

What I've learned about Darren's diabetes

PROVEN REPEATEDLYTime in range has stayed well above the 70% goal across recent reviews.
LIKELY1:15 remains the best-supported working ICR. This review shows no clear post-meal low pattern suggesting it is too strong.
LIKELYHigh-fat/high-carb and closely spaced evening eating are more likely to produce delayed or extended rises.
UNDER INVESTIGATIONBread and other rapid starch meals may have their own response pattern, but the current evidence is not clean enough to justify changing the global ICR.
ANALYSIS RULESmall-carb meals rounded up to a whole insulin unit can be effectively much stronger than 1:15 and must be monitored separately rather than used to judge the global ratio.
ANALYSIS RULEOverlapping meals, unmatched Dexcom doses, documented sensor faults and unclear nutrition are excluded from clean ICR evidence.

Version history

v2.8.0 - Expanded restaurant database + safe demo
  • Expanded restaurant nutrition to 436 verified entries across Greggs, PizzaExpress and McDonald's UK.
  • Restaurant cards now show source freshness and limited-menu flags.
  • Added an isolated local-only demo mode for sharing the Meal Tracker and restaurant builder without Darren's cloud, Nightscout or CGM Progress data.
  • Restaurant meal basket and one-time insulin rounding remain unchanged.
v2.6.2 - Collapsible food library
  • My food library is now collapsed by default to keep the Meal Tracker shorter and cleaner.
  • Tap Show library or the section header to expand the full saved-food list.
  • The open/closed choice is remembered on this device.
  • Favourite foods remain available separately for quick access without opening the full library.
v2.6.1 - Mobile usability tidy-up
  • Nightscout connection settings now collapse automatically once a working connection is saved.
  • Added a Connection settings toggle so the URL and read-only token stay out of the way during normal use.
  • Favourite foods now prioritise recently used items, support direct favourite search, and can expand from the quick top six to the full list.
  • Added a persistent Hide favourites control to reduce vertical scrolling on phones.
v2.6.0 - Live CGM Intelligence
  • Added rolling 3h/6h/12h/24h CGM trend graph with meal markers.
  • Added Right Now dashboard with current glucose, trend, in-range streak and last logged meal.
  • Added retrospective meal outcome cards from Nightscout history: start, peak, rise, peak time, 2h/4h/6h, time above 10, lows and delayed rises.
  • Added personal meal memory, pattern detection, ICR evidence summary, low/high event view and personalised learning panel.
  • Fresh Nightscout source timestamp is tagged into new meal notes.
v2.5.1 - Live Nightscout CGM
  • Added direct read-only Nightscout live CGM support for current glucose, trend and reading age.
  • Fresh Nightscout readings can fill the meal glucose and trend fields while keeping manual override.
  • The app refreshes the live feed on demand, when returning to the app and every 60 seconds.
  • Read-only Nightscout credentials are stored only in local browser storage on the device and are never committed to GitHub or saved to Supabase.
  • Readings older than 10 minutes are flagged stale and are not offered for meal auto-fill.
v2.5.0 - CGM trend capture / pre-bolus evidence
  • Added a required Dexcom CGM trend-arrow field beside the current glucose reading.
  • New meals now capture rising, stable or falling direction for later matched CGM analysis.
  • The trend is preserved in Supabase-compatible notes and exported as its own CSV column without requiring a database migration.
  • Bolus timing remains observation-only: the app does not yet recommend a specific number of minutes before eating.
  • Future 3-file reviews will use starting glucose + trend + meal type + carbs + fat + actual timing to build personalised pre-bolus evidence.
v2.4.9 - 2 Sep CGM review
  • Processed the complete three-file review through 2 Sep 2026.
  • Updated CGM Progress to TIR 85%, average 7.7 mmol/L, GMI 6.6% and CV 28.8%.
  • Retained 1:15 as the working ICR: no clear post-meal low signal supports winding back to 1:20, and current confounded highs do not justify strengthening it.
  • Added explicit monitoring of whole-unit round-up on small-carb meals and later-evening highs.
v2.4.8 - favourite persistence fix
  • Fixed Favourite toggles being overwritten by the previous saved state.
  • Favourite and unfavourite changes now write directly to Supabase when online.
  • Offline favourite changes are queued and update the local library immediately.
v2.4.8 - favourite foods quick access
  • Added a Favourite foods panel for one-tap reuse of starred foods.
  • Added All foods / Favourites filters to the personal food library.
  • Favourite status remains cloud-synced across signed-in devices.
